Welcome to N0CAPE. This utility dynamically extracts and pairs network interfaces with their respective switch hostnames from raw data.

> Instructions (01)

Provide the engine with raw data (Ticket descriptions, CDP/LLDP outputs, or Inventory tables). As long as the text contains a Switch Hostname (e.g., CO-ESP-ACC02M02) and Port Numbers (e.g., gi1/0/3), the tool will map them.

Supported Formats: It supports single ports, comma-separated shorthand (Gi3/0/1,3,5), dashed ranges (Gi2/0/23 - Gi2/0/27), reverse tables (29 SW-NAME 2), and direct-attached formatting (SW-NAME - 1/0/2). It gracefully auto-corrects Hostname typos (e.g., CASO702 -> CAS0702) and silently fixes common port prefix typos like 61 or Gl.

Smart Directional Mapping: The engine automatically detects your log's writing style. If it detects ports are listed before the switch (Bottom-Up format), it intelligently flips its scanning direction so they are flawlessly mapped together without any "Gravity Bleed" from other switches. It also safely ignores trailing FQDNs from hostnames.

Interactive Filtering & Grouping: Click any switch tag in the top right target bar to instantly isolate its mapped interfaces! Toggle "SUMMARIZE SH RUN" to automatically group sequential port commands into a single regex format. The engine also auto-highlights layout tags (Prod, Training).
